# Launch checklist for an actual SaaS website

- Replace ATLAS and FIELDNOTE with your own brand and product references.
- Replace every synthetic UI figure, testimonial-like snippet and example plan price with factual content.
- Decide whether each pricing CTA leads to a tested checkout, a working lead form, or a contact page. The default demo does **not** accept payments or leads.
- Add appropriate legal pages, privacy notices, terms and cookie handling for your actual site.
- Configure and test the real integrations, analytics, and consent tools your website uses.
- Deploy the edited HTML to HTTPS static hosting under a domain you control.
- Test desktop/mobile layouts, keyboard navigation, reduced motion, screen-reader behavior and accessible contrast.
- Test all links, forms, privacy features, security and conversion paths in the deployed version.
- Confirm legal rights for any images, fonts, logos or third-party code you add.
- Recheck the Agentrix Free Use License and do not redistribute the original template pack as a paid competing kit.
