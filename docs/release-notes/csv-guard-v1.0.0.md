# CSV Guard 1.0.0 | Check CSV files for missing columns and formatting errors

**A CSV import fails because rows have different numbers of columns or broken quotation marks.**

## Who this is for
People importing spreadsheet exports into business systems.

## What you can do
Checks a CSV file locally and points to possible formatting mistakes. You can optionally check cells that a spreadsheet might interpret as formulas.

## Get started
[Open this free tool's page](https://github.com/pauloflp/Agentrix/blob/main/products/agentrix-csv-guard/README.md) to follow a real example. It runs locally and does not require a subscription or online account. The source is MIT-licensed.

## Important to know
It checks structure; it does not clean, convert or upload your data.
