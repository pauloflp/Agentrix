# UTM Audit 0.1.0 | Check campaign tracking links for missing UTM tags

**Links used in advertisements or emails have missing or inconsistent UTM labels.**

## Who this is for
Marketers and developers checking links before sending campaigns.

## What you can do
Checks a list of URLs locally and flags common tracking inconsistencies.

## Get started
[Open this free tool's page](https://github.com/pauloflp/Agentrix/blob/main/products/agentrix-utm-audit/README.md) to follow a real example. It runs locally and does not require a subscription or online account. The source is MIT-licensed.

## Important to know
It checks tags, not ad performance or whether a destination URL is online.
